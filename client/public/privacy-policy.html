Privacy Policy for hireReady-AI

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

hireReady-AI — When Talent Meets Intelligence ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is handled. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, storage, and processing of personal information when using the hireReady-AI mobile application and web platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide personalized career acceleration services, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Credentials: Email address and encrypted password hash used for secure authentication.
  • Professional & Profile Data: Full name, target role, education, college, graduation year, target skills, location, and professional links (LinkedIn, portfolio).
  • Resume & Career Information: Work history, education, project highlights, achievements, and certifications you enter or edit in the ATS Resume Builder.
  • Interview & Assessment Inputs: Mock interview responses, practice transcripts, and AI-generated feedback.
  • Application Pipeline: Company names, job titles, application URLs, statuses, and notes saved in your tracker.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for functional and service delivery purposes:

  • Building, formatting, and exporting ATS-compliant resumes.
  • Generating real-time AI interview simulations and constructive performance analysis.
  • Computing skill gap breakdowns and personalized learning roadmaps.
  • Synchronizing your career data securely across your devices.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Third-Party Processors

hireReady-AI uses secure AI inference engines (such as Groq and Gemini) to generate career mentorship, resume suggestions, and interview feedback. Your prompt inputs are sent securely via TLS/HTTPS encryption solely to produce requested responses. Your data is not sold to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Security

All sensitive credentials (such as passwords) are salted and hashed using bcrypt. All network communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. Local data stored on your device utilizes standard sandboxed storage provided by Android and web security standards.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may update, edit, or remove your resume and profile information at any time directly through the app interface. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data permanently, you can submit a deletion request via our support email.

6. Children's Privacy

hireReady-AI is designed for job seekers, students, and working professionals. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
